Formulary Editor

Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Formulary Editor is a Chrome extension for NHS formulary editors. It helps them write the Further Information of entries on the Norfolk and Waveney and the Ipswich and East Suffolk NetFormulary sites.

What it reads

The extension only runs on the editing pages (/formadmin/) of those NetFormulary sites. There, it reads the entry being edited: its Further Information text and a few details from the same page, such as the drug name, so it can show them in the editor. For Preview, and to look up entries linked from "Also in the formulary", it loads the public formulary pages of the same site. It does not read any other website.

What it keeps

While you edit, the extension keeps a backup of changes you have not yet applied, so you do not lose work if the page closes. It also remembers whether "Advanced tools" is switched on. Both are stored only in your own browser, in the extension's local storage. Backups are deleted when they are no longer needed, such as when you choose Start again or Discard, and after 30 days. Removing the extension deletes them all.

What it sends

Nothing. The extension has no analytics, advertising, tracking or accounts, and no server. It does not send what you type, or anything about you, to the developer or to anyone else. Changes reach NetFormulary only when you apply them and then press Save on NetFormulary's own page, in the same way as editing there directly.

Personal and patient information

The extension does not collect personal information. Formulary entries are prescribing guidance and should not contain patient information.
